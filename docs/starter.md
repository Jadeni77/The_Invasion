# Main

This game is a pixel-style tower defense game where players are a character who need to protect 
their garden from the attack of little evil creatures

# Game

- Lobby:
    - Upper side with 3 boxes (Collection, Achievement, Setting) and an energy bar representing the energy the player has left
        - Collection: display all the evil creatures and resources the user has defeated and collected
        - Achievement: bars of achievement that the player can earn
        - Setting: end game(during game), volume? Sound? Sound Effect? Signout? Switch Account? What else?
    - The middle part shows a map where the user can click on to enter a level (pvz version 2)
        - During game
            - First, select 6 cards they want to use for this level
            - The 6 cards will shuffle randomly and only show 3 cards, while rest of the cards are awaiting for the previous card to be use and then reshuffle (consider clash of royale for card shuffling)
            - Maybe show the garden, then use the 3 cards to put on top of the garden, and the player in front of the garden.
            - Little creatures start to come through the woods and try to attack
            - The player can use the card they have to clear the creature waves
            - If the creature reached the garden, then the player lost
    - The bottom part is where the user can use the resources to upgrade the card they have
        - The upper part has a sort where users can filter the cards that are shown, and also the materials they have earned
        - Then the user can click on the card to see the information about the card and whether they have enough resources to upgrade it or not
